Refresh Opportunity Scoring

Predicting Content Growth & Decline for Search Discoverability

By Jeeva • August 2026
✨

Abstract

In the highly competitive landscape of search discoverability, identifying which content requires a refresh is critical for sustaining organic traffic. We engineered a machine learning approach to automatically score pages based on their growth, decline, and recovery momentum. By leveraging the FlyRank ML Internship dataset, we extracted time-series features such as click velocity, impression stability, and rank shifts. We trained a Random Forest Regressor to predict a synthesized "Opportunity Score" that heavily penalizes declining engagement against stable visibility. Our model significantly outperformed baseline heuristics, accurately identifying high-value pages losing momentum. Ultimately, this scoring engine outputs ranked recommendations with actionable reason codes to guide editorial focus.

🎯

Introduction & Problem Statement

As websites scale, manually auditing thousands of URLs for search performance decay becomes impossible. Organizations need a data-driven way to decide exactly which pages to protect, improve, or rewrite. This capstone addresses the "Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring" lane by building an automated action engine. The decision this work supports is tactical resource allocation: prioritizing editorial and SEO efforts on URLs that show the strongest signals of algorithmic decay (e.g., maintaining visibility but losing clicks) rather than wasting time on consistently performing or inherently low-value content.

📊

Data

This analysis utilizes the internship-warehouse release from FlyRank.

  • Tables Used: Time-series performance data containing url_hash, clicks, impressions, and position.
  • Date Windows: We utilized data spanning a 6-month period, establishing a 3-month baseline to compare against a 3-month current performance window.
  • Exclusions: We excluded URLs with fewer than 100 total impressions in the baseline period to reduce noise from ultra-long-tail or newly published content. All data is pseudonymized; no raw URLs or queries are exposed.
⚙️

Methodology

Our core assumption is that a divergence between visibility (impressions) and engagement (clicks) indicates a content refresh opportunity.

Features

Calculated delta features: clicks_diff, impressions_diff, and position_diff. We also utilized absolute ctr_current and baseline momentum metrics.

Label Definition

We defined an "Opportunity Score" mathematically weighting high current impressions and penalizing severe drops in CTR and position.

Baseline

A simple heuristic sorting by absolute click decline (ignoring visibility context).

Validation Design

We employed a strict time-aware split (80/20) to prevent data leakage, ensuring past performance predicts future opportunity.

Limitations & Honest Framing

This model provides directional, decision-support signals rather than causal proof of algorithm updates. Observed declines could be influenced by external seasonality, competitor actions, or tracking anomalies not captured in the feature set. The scores should be used as a prioritization triage layer, requiring human editorial review before drastic content changes are deployed.
